# Fortify logo: Layered F

The F the team already knows, with its middle arm lifted onto its own glass layer, set on an Apple-style squircle tile.
Everything here is generated by `build_kit.py` from one geometry source. Edit the geometry there and do not hand-edit the SVGs.

## Construction

- 256 grid. The stem is 48 wide and the horizontals are 46 tall (thinned slightly to look optically equal). The outer corner has a radius of 32, matching the tile curve.
- The middle arm sits 12 units off the stem. On the tile, that gap and a 72 % opacity make up the layer. In one-colour versions, the gap alone carries it.
- The glyph sits 4 units right of the geometric centre because the stem carries the visual weight.
- On the tile, the glyph is 66 % of the tile (small cut: 74 %). The tile is a superellipse with n = 5.
- Wordmark: Inter Variable 600, tracking −0.02 em, outlined to paths, in Ink. It stays neutral, per the One Blue Rule.
- Horizontal lockup: the cap height is ½ the tile and the gap is ¼ the tile. Stacked lockup: the cap height is 56/256 of the tile and the gap is 48/256.

## Colour

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Action Blue | `oklch(57.72% 0.2324 260)` ≈ `#056DFF` | Flat symbol on light backgrounds, mono version |
| Pressed Blue | `oklch(48.8% 0.243 264.4)` ≈ `#1447E6` | Bottom of the tile gradient, theme-color |
| Tile top | `oklch(66% 0.19 258)` ≈ `#3E8FFF` | Top of the tile gradient |
| Ink | `oklch(20.5% 0 0)` = `#171717` | Wordmark |

The hex values are sRGB clips of the OKLCH tokens. CMYK and Pantone matches still need a print proof.

## Rules

- **Clear space:** the stem width (48/256 of the tile) on every side of the tile or lockup.
- **Minimum size:** the tile is 16 px on screen and 6 mm in print. Use the small cut only for favicons that really render at 16–32 device pixels. UI logomarks at 24 CSS px and up keep the full Layered F, because they render at 48 px or more on retina screens. The horizontal lockup has a minimum height of 20 px.
- **Backgrounds:** use the tile on any neutral. Use the flat white symbol on Action Blue or on dark. Never put the tile on a busy photo.
- **Don't:** recolour the tile outside the blue ramp, close the layer gap on large sizes, add a shadow under the glyph, set the wordmark in blue, or stretch the squircle.

## Tested

The marks were rendered and checked at 16, 32, 64, 96 and 512 px, in one colour, reversed, and on the six presentation mockups (`preview.html`, `slides/`). Below 32 px the 12-unit gap closes, which is why the small cut exists.

## Open items

- Run a trademark search (USPTO, reverse image search) before any external use. A plain F on a blue tile is common.
- Inter is under the SIL OFL, which allows logo use.
