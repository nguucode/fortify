What we heard

The brief

Fortify is the CRM a US independent P&C agency uses to sell, service and retain personal and commercial lines. The mark moves the old blue 'F' tile into an Apple-style app icon without losing the equity staff already recognise.

calmpreciseprotectivequiet

Keep the blue F tile equity, read at 16 px, avoid insurance clichés (shields, umbrellas, houses), sit inside the One Blue Rule.

Concept A

Layered F

The F the team already knows, its middle arm lifted onto its own glass layer.

  • Keeps the blue F tile equity; nobody has to relearn the app
  • The floating arm is the one ownable detail: layered cover, Apple-style depth
  • Outer corner shares the tile's curve; a solid small cut holds at 16 px
